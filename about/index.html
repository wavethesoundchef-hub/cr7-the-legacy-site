About this archive

A tribute, kept with care

An independent archive of the life, career, honours and influence of Cristiano Ronaldo: told as a documentary, kept as a reference.

The project

There are two ways through the archive. The Story follows his life in chapters, from Madeira to the present day. The Archive holds the record itself: clubs, seasons, trophies, awards, records and statistics.

The two are woven together. Every chapter points to the evidence behind it, and every honour and record leads back to the moment it was earned.

Sources & methodology

  1. 01

    Every fact has a source

    Each statistic, record, trophy and date in the archive is stored with at least one source, and that source is shown alongside it.

  2. 02

    Verified, not assumed

    We prefer primary and official records: club, league and federation archives, and contemporary match reports. Where reputable sources disagree, we say so rather than choosing silently.

  3. 03

    Nothing ahead of the facts

    The archive documents what has happened. Events are added only once they are confirmed, never in anticipation.

  4. 04

    Corrections are welcome

    Archives improve through scrutiny. A public corrections channel opens when the archive launches; every correction will be checked against its evidence before anything changes.

Images

Photographs come from openly licensed collections such as Wikimedia Commons, and every image is credited with its photographer and licence. Some professional photographs are shown through Getty Images' embed service, inside Getty's own viewer. No official club, competition or brand logos are used as design elements, and no photograph is ever generated or altered to show something that did not happen.
