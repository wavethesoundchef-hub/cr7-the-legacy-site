#On the record

Key dates

  1. 12 August 2003

    Manchester United completed his signing on 12 August 2003: aged 18, on a five-year contract, for a €17.5m fee payable over two years. Alex Ferguson said his players had urged him to sign Ronaldo after the Sporting friendly.

    • UEFA · United complete double signing · 12 August 2003
  2. 16 August 2003

    Made his Manchester United debut on 16 August 2003 against Bolton, coming off the bench.

    • UEFA · Memories of Ronaldo's debut: on this day in 2002 · 14 August 2017
  3. —

    Scored 42 goals in 2007/08 as Manchester United won the league ahead of Chelsea.

    • Manchester United · 2000 – 2009 | History by Decade
  4. 27 August 2021

    Manchester United announced agreement with Juventus for his transfer on 27 August 2021, subject to personal terms, visa and medical.

    • Manchester United · United confirm Ronaldo agreement · 27 August 2021
  5. 11 September 2021

    Marked his return with two goals as Manchester United beat Newcastle 4-1 at Old Trafford on 11 September 2021: one on the stroke of half-time, the other six minutes after Newcastle's 56th-minute equaliser.

    • Manchester United · Report: United 4 Newcastle 1 · 11 September 2021
  6. 22 November 2022

    Left Manchester United by mutual agreement, with immediate effect, on 22 November 2022.

    • Premier League · Cristiano Ronaldo leaves Man Utd with immediate effect · 22 November 2022
