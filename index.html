He headed the opening goal after 26 minutes and missed his kick in the shoot-out.Open the moment →
  3. 10 July 2016Portugal1–0Champions from the touchlineWon UEFA EURO 2016 with Portugal: 1-0 against France after extra time at the Stade de France on 10 July 2016. He went off injured after 25 minutes; Éder scored in the 109th minute.Open the moment →
  4. 3 April 2018Real Madrid64'The overhead kick in TurinOn 3 April 2018 he scored twice, including a 64th-minute overhead kick, as Real Madrid beat Juventus 3-0 in Turin in the Champions League quarter-final first leg. The overhead kick prompted even the home fans to applaud.Open the moment →
