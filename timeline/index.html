  1. 1985

    1. Photo: Banja-Frans Mulder·CC BY 3.0·Commons
      5 February · Madeira

      Born in Funchal

      Born on 5 February 1985.

    The academy years

    At 11, Sporting signed him from Nacional after a trial in Lisbon; at 12 he moved to the capital to live at the club's academy.

    Chapter I →

  2. 2001

    1. During the year · Portugal

      He played for Portugal's under-17 team in 2001-02, wearing the No. 7, before moving up to the under-21s.

  3. 2002

    1. 14 August · Sporting CP

      Debut at 17

      Made his Sporting CP debut aged 17 on 14 August 2002, coming on for Toñito with nearly an hour gone against Inter in the UEFA Champions League third qualifying round, first leg (0-0).

    2. 29 September · Sporting CP

      Played his first league game on 29 September 2002, a 4-2 defeat at Braga.

    3. 7 October · Sporting CP

      Scored his first senior competitive goal on 7 October 2002, in a league game against Moreirense, and added a second.

  4. 2003

    1. 12 August · Sporting CP

      Signed by Manchester United

      Manchester United completed his signing on 12 August 2003: aged 18, on a five-year contract, for a €17.5m fee payable over two years. Alex Ferguson said his players had urged him to sign Ronaldo after the Sporting friendly.

    2. 16 August · Manchester United

      Number 7 at Old Trafford

      Made his Manchester United debut on 16 August 2003 against Bolton, coming off the bench.

    3. 20 August · Portugal

      Made his senior Portugal debut on 20 August 2003 in a 1-0 friendly win over Kazakhstan.

    4. 18 November · Portugal

      On 18 November 2003, in the second leg of the Under-21 Championship play-off in France, he scored Portugal's first goal and converted the decisive penalty in the shoot-out that sent them to the 2004 finals.

  5. 2004

    1. 12 June · Portugal

      First goal for Portugal

      Scored his first goal for Portugal on 12 June 2004, at UEFA Euro 2004.

    2. 4 July · Portugal

      At EURO 2004 he scored twice, against Greece and in the semi-final against the Netherlands, as Portugal reached the final, which they lost 1-0 to Greece in Lisbon. He was named in the Team of the Tournament.

    3. During the year · Portugal

      He played for Portugal at the Athens 2004 Olympic Games, wearing the No. 7.

    4. During the year · Manchester United

      Won the 2004 FA Cup with Manchester United: 3-0 against Millwall at the Millennium Stadium in Cardiff, with one goal from Ronaldo and two from Ruud van Nistelrooy.

    5. 2003/04 season · Manchester United

      Manchester United's Sir Matt Busby Player of the Year (1 of 4).

  6. 2006

    1. During the year · Portugal

      At the 2006 World Cup in Germany he scored against IR Iran and converted a penalty in the quarter-final shoot-out win over England; Portugal reached the semi-finals for the first time in 40 years and lost to France.

  7. 2007

    1. 2006/07 season · Manchester United

      Won the PFA Player of the Year and PFA Young Player of the Year in 2006/07, among 13 personal honours that season.

    2. 2006/07 season · Manchester United

      Manchester United's Sir Matt Busby Player of the Year (2 of 4).

  8. 2008

    1. 9 April · Manchester United

      As ambassador of UEFA’s Score for the Red Cross campaign at EURO 2008, he was presented with a CHF 100,000 cheque for land-mine victims in Afghanistan, funding artificial limbs, physiotherapy and vocational training.

    2. 21 May · Manchester United

      Moscow, decided on penalties

      Won the 2008 Champions League final with Manchester United: 1-1 against Chelsea after extra time at the Luzhniki Stadium, Moscow, and 6-5 on penalties. He headed the opening goal after 26 minutes and missed his kick in the shoot-out.

    3. 19 June · Portugal

      At EURO 2008 he scored once, against the Czech Republic; Portugal went out in the quarter-finals, 3-2 to Germany.

    4. Photo: Ank Kumar·CC BY-SA 4.0·Commons
      During the year · Manchester United

      The first Ballon d'Or

      Won the Ballon d'Or (1 of 5).

    5. During the year · Manchester United

      UEFA's top individual player award (1 of 4).

    6. During the year · Manchester United

      Won the 2008 FIFA Club World Cup with Manchester United, 1-0 against LDU Quito in the final: Wayne Rooney scored in the 73rd minute after Ronaldo rolled the ball into his path, with United down to ten men.

    7. 2007/08 season · Manchester United

      Won the European Golden Shoe (1 of 4).

    8. 2007/08 season · Manchester United

      Manchester United's Sir Matt Busby Player of the Year (3 of 4).

  9. 2009

    1. 6 July · Real Madrid

      The Bernabéu fills for him

      Unveiled at the Santiago Bernabéu on 6 July 2009, joined by Eusébio and Alfredo Di Stéfano.

    2. 29 August · Real Madrid

      Made his Real Madrid debut on 29 August 2009 in a 3-2 win over Deportivo, scoring.

  10. 2010

    1. 28 October · Real Madrid

      A Nike athlete: in October 2010 he and Nike launched his signature boot, the CR Mercurial Vapor SuperFly II, in Madrid; in June 2026 he featured in Nike's 'Rip the Script' football campaign.

    2. During the year · Portugal

      At the 2010 World Cup in South Africa, his first as Portugal captain, he scored in the 7-0 win over Korea DPR; Portugal lost to Spain in the Round of 16.

  11. 2011

    1. 2010/11 season · Real Madrid

      Won the European Golden Shoe (2 of 4).

  12. 2012

    1. 27 June · Portugal

      At EURO 2012 he scored three times, twice against the Netherlands and the winner in the quarter-final against the Czech Republic; Portugal lost the semi-final to Spain on penalties after a 0-0 draw.

  13. 2013

    1. 4 June · Real Madrid

      Herbalife became his official nutrition sponsor in June 2013, on a five-year, multi-million-dollar contract including global publicity rights and the chance to develop co-branded sports nutrition products.

    2. Photo: Tkotw12·CC BY-SA 4.0·Commons
      15 December · Madeira

      A museum in his home town

      The CR7 Museum in Funchal, his home town, opened on 15 December 2013. It shows more than a hundred individual and team trophies from his career.

    3. During the year · Real Madrid

      CR7 Underwear, his underwear line, is manufactured by JBS Textile Group of Denmark; it launched in Europe in 2013 and in North America in late 2017.

    4. During the year · Real Madrid

      Won the Ballon d'Or (2 of 5).

  14. 2014

    1. During the year · Portugal

      At the 2014 World Cup in Brazil, slowed by a knee injury, he scored the winner against Ghana, but Portugal went out in the group stage.

    2. Photo: Anish Morarji from St Albans, England·CC BY 2.0·Commons
      During the year · Real Madrid

      A third Ballon d'Or

      Won the Ballon d'Or (3 of 5).

    3. During the year · Real Madrid

      UEFA's top individual player award (2 of 4).

    4. During the year · Real Madrid

      Won the UEFA Champions League with Real Madrid (2 of 5).

    5. 2013/14 season · Real Madrid

      Won the European Golden Shoe (3 of 4).

  15. 2015

    1. 20 April · Real Madrid

      Scored a hat-trick in the 12th Match Against Poverty in Saint-Étienne in April 2015, whose proceeds largely went to helping Guinea, Liberia and Sierra Leone recover from the Ebola epidemic.

    2. September · Real Madrid

      Launched his debut fragrance, Cristiano Ronaldo Legacy, in September 2015; the official Cristiano Ronaldo Fragrances store is run by Eden Parfums Ltd.

    3. Photo: Dr. Thomas Liptak·CC BY-SA 4.0·Commons
      During the year · Real Madrid

      Pestana CR7 is born

      Pestana CR7 Lifestyle Hotels is a partnership, established in 2015, between the Pestana Hotel Group and Cristiano Ronaldo: he contributes the CR7 brand and image and is an investor, while Pestana runs the hotels. Funchal and Lisbon opened in 2016, Madrid and New York in 2021 and Marrakech in 2022; Paris is planned for 2027.

    4. 2014/15 season · Real Madrid

      Won the European Golden Shoe (4 of 4).

  16. 2016

    1. 10 July · Portugal

      Champions of Europe

      Won UEFA EURO 2016 with Portugal: 1-0 against France after extra time at the Stade de France on 10 July 2016. He went off injured after 25 minutes; Éder scored in the 109th minute.

    2. 19 December · Real Madrid

      In December 2016 the US gym chain Crunch announced CR7 fitness clubs with him: a franchise venture for Spain, starting in Madrid.

    3. During the year · Real Madrid

      Won the Ballon d'Or (4 of 5).

    4. During the year · Real Madrid

      UEFA's top individual player award (3 of 4).

    5. During the year · Real Madrid

      Won the UEFA Champions League with Real Madrid (3 of 5).

  17. 2017

    1. 29 March · Madeira

      On 29 March 2017 Madeira's international airport took his name, at a ceremony led by President Marcelo Rebelo de Sousa, attended by Prime Minister António Costa, at which he spoke. It is now Madeira International Airport Cristiano Ronaldo.

    2. During the year · Real Madrid

      Won the Ballon d'Or (5 of 5).

    3. During the year · Real Madrid

      UEFA's top individual player award (4 of 4).

    4. During the year · Real Madrid

      Won the UEFA Champions League with Real Madrid (4 of 5).

  18. 2018

    1. 3 April · Real Madrid

      The overhead kick in Turin

      On 3 April 2018 he scored twice, including a 64th-minute overhead kick, as Real Madrid beat Juventus 3-0 in Turin in the Champions League quarter-final first leg. The overhead kick prompted even the home fans to applaud.

    2. 10 July · Juventus

      To Juventus

      Juventus agreed his permanent transfer from Real Madrid on 10 July 2018 for €100 million payable over two financial years, plus €12 million in additional costs, on a four-year contract to 30 June 2022.

    3. 16 July · Juventus

      Officially presented as a Juventus player on 16 July 2018.

    4. 16 September · Juventus

      Scored his first two Juventus goals, in the 50th and 65th minutes, in a 2-1 win over Sassuolo on 16 September 2018.

    5. During the year · Portugal

      At the 2018 World Cup in Russia he scored four goals, a hat-trick in the 3-3 draw with Spain and the winner against Morocco; Portugal lost 2-1 to Uruguay in the Round of 16.

    6. Photo: Fotografías Archimadrid.es·CC BY-SA 2.0·Commons
      During the year · Real Madrid

      A fifth European Cup

      Won the UEFA Champions League with Real Madrid (5 of 5).

  19. 2019

    1. 16 January · Juventus

      Scored the only goal, a 61st-minute header, as Juventus beat AC Milan 1-0 in Jeddah on 16 January 2019 to win the Italian Super Cup, the club's eighth.

    2. 20 April · Juventus

      Juventus clinched the 2018/19 Serie A title, their eighth in a row, with a 2-1 win over Fiorentina on 20 April 2019.

    3. 5 June · Portugal

      Scored a hat-trick, including two in the last three minutes, as Portugal beat Switzerland 3-1 in the 2019 Nations League semi-final on 5 June 2019.

    4. 9 June · Portugal

      Portugal won the first UEFA Nations League, 1-0 against the Netherlands at the Estádio do Dragão.

  20. 2020

    1. March · Juventus

      In March 2020 he and his agent Jorge Mendes paid for intensive care units in Portugal: two new ten-bed units at the Northern Lisbon University Hospital Centre and a new unit at Santo Antonio Hospital in Porto, with beds, ventilators and heart-rate monitors.

    2. 26 July · Juventus

      He scored as Juventus beat Sampdoria 2-0 on 26 July 2020 to clinch the 2019/20 Serie A title, their ninth in a row.

  21. 2021

    1. 20 January · Juventus

      He opened the scoring as Juventus beat Napoli 2-0 on 20 January 2021 to win the Italian Super Cup for the ninth time.

    2. 19 May · Juventus

      Juventus beat Atalanta 2-1 on 19 May 2021 to win the Italian Cup (their 14th). He started; his back heel set up a Chiesa shot that hit the post.

    3. 27 June · Portugal

      At EURO 2020, played in 2021, he scored five goals, twice against Hungary, once against Germany and twice against France; Portugal lost 1-0 to Belgium in the Round of 16.

    4. 27 August · The Return

      Manchester United announced agreement with Juventus for his transfer on 27 August 2021, subject to personal terms, visa and medical.

    5. 31 August · Juventus

      Left Juventus on 31 August 2021 after three years, 133 appearances, 101 goals and five trophies.

    6. 1 September · Portugal

      Past Ali Daei

      Became the outright record scorer in men's international football on 1 September 2021, with two late goals that turned defeat into a win against the Republic of Ireland, taking him to 111, two more than Ali Daei.

    7. Photo: Ardfern·CC BY-SA 4.0·Commons
      11 September · The Return

      Back at Old Trafford

      Marked his return with two goals as Manchester United beat Newcastle 4-1 at Old Trafford on 11 September 2021: one on the stroke of half-time, the other six minutes after Newcastle's 56th-minute equaliser.

    8. 2020/21 season · Juventus

      Serie A top scorer in 2020/21, with 29 goals.

  22. 2022

    1. Photo: Ardfern·CC BY-SA 4.0·Commons
      12 March · The Return

      Hat-trick against Spurs

      Scored a hat-trick, the winner a header nine minutes from time, as Manchester United beat Tottenham 3-2 on 12 March 2022: his first Premier League hat-trick in 14 years and 59 days.

    2. November · The Return

      The first person to reach 500 million followers on Instagram, in November 2022.

    3. 22 November · The Return

      Left Manchester United by mutual agreement, with immediate effect, on 22 November 2022.

    4. 24 November · Portugal

      At the 2022 World Cup he became the first man to score at 10 consecutive international tournaments; the run ended at EURO 2024.

    5. During the year · Portugal

      At the 2022 World Cup in Qatar his penalty against Ghana made him the first man to score at five World Cups; Portugal reached the quarter-finals and lost 1-0 to Morocco.

    6. 2021/22 season · The Return

      Manchester United's Sir Matt Busby Player of the Year (4 of 4).

  23. 2023

    1. 3 February · Al Nassr

      Scored his first Saudi Pro League goal on 3 February 2023, a penalty three minutes into injury time at Al Fateh that rescued a 2-2 draw, in his second league match.

    2. 20 June · Portugal

      The first to 200

      Became the first player to reach 200 international appearances, against Iceland in June 2023, marking it with an 89th-minute winner.

    3. During the year · Al Nassr

      Won the King Salman Club Cup with Al Nassr in the summer of 2023.

  24. 2024

    1. 27 May · Al Nassr

      Thirty-five

      Scored 35 Saudi Pro League goals in 2023-24, the most in a single season, beating Abderrazak Hamdallah's 34 from 2018-19. He went into the final round on 33 and broke the record with a header against Al Ittihad on 27 May 2024.

    2. 5 July · Portugal

      At EURO 2024, a record sixth EURO for him, he did not score; Portugal went out in the quarter-finals to France on penalties after a 0-0 draw.

    3. August · Al Nassr

      Holds the Guinness World Records title for the most YouTube subscribers gained in 24 hours: 19,729,827. By August 2024 his following across social media reached nearly one billion accounts.

    4. September · Al Nassr

      In September 2024 he became the first person to reach one billion followers across social media platforms.

    5. 2023/24 season · Al Nassr

      Saudi Pro League top scorer (1 of 2).

  25. 2025

    1. 15 May · Al Nassr

      Named the world’s highest-paid athlete by Forbes in 2025 for the third year in a row and the fifth time overall, with estimated earnings of $275 million over 12 months, on and off the field, before taxes and agent fees.

    2. 8 June · Portugal

      A final at 40

      Volleyed Portugal's second equaliser in the 61st minute of the 2025 Nations League final against Spain, aged 40: his 138th international goal.

    3. 8 June · Portugal

      Portugal won the UEFA Nations League again, beating Spain 5-3 on penalties after a 2-2 draw: the first team to win it twice.

    4. 26 June · Al Nassr

      Extended his Al Nassr contract until 2027, announced on 26 June 2025.

    5. 2024/25 season · Al Nassr

      Saudi Pro League top scorer (2 of 2).

  26. 2026

    1. 18 February · Al Nassr

      In February 2026 he invested $7.5 million to acquire a 10% equity stake in HBL Pro2col Software, a Herbalife technology company, alongside a commitment to provide services and sponsorship rights.

    2. 21 May · Al Nassr

      Champion in a fourth country

      Won the 2025-26 Saudi Pro League with Al Nassr, his first league title in Saudi Arabia and the club's first in seven years, sealed with a double in a 4-1 win over Damac on 21 May 2026.

    3. 23 June · Portugal

      A goal at six World Cups

      Became the first player to score at six World Cups with the opening goal of Portugal's 5-0 win over Uzbekistan in Houston on 23 June 2026. He scored twice that day; the second, his 10th World Cup goal, made him Portugal's leading scorer at World Cups, ahead of Eusébio's nine.

    4. July · Al Nassr

      At the 2026 World Cup he signed two footballs, after Portugal's win over Croatia, for two young brothers with muscular dystrophy who had written to him.

    5. 5 November · Portugal

      234 and 146

      Announced his retirement from international football on 5 November 2026.

    6. During the year · Portugal

      At the 2026 World Cup in North America he scored three goals, two against Uzbekistan and the equaliser against Croatia in Toronto; Portugal went out to Spain in the Round of 16, and he said it was his last World Cup.
